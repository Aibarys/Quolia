Quolia is a quiet reading app for iPhone and iPad. Read your own books in a calm reader, highlight what matters, write down your thoughts — and get them back when it counts.

YOUR BOOKS, ANY FORMAT
• EPUB, FB2 (including .fb2.zip), PDF and TXT.
• Import from Files and iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Open In and the Share sheet.
• Tap a footnote mark to read the note right on the page.
• Ads that download sites add to books are removed automatically.

CALM READING
• Pages or scrolling, with slide or curl page turns.
• Font, size, spacing, text width, alignment and hyphenation.
• Light, Sepia, Dark and Black OLED themes — even for PDFs.
• Page of the whole book, and time left in the chapter and the book.
• Focus Mode keeps the current line or paragraph bright.
• Auto Scroll at your own speed, and Reading Flow for distraction-free sessions.
• Listen to the book with the spoken paragraph highlighted.

HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES
• Five colors, notes and tags for any passage.
• Continue a selection onto the next page.
• All your quotes, notes, bookmarks and saved words in one place, with search and filters.
• Export to Markdown (for Notes, Obsidian, Notion) or PDF.
• Beautiful quote cards for stories and posts.

REMEMBER
• Quolia brings your highlights back with spaced repetition: "6 months ago you highlighted this".
• An optional daily reminder.

DICTIONARY AND AI
• Meaning, translation and pronunciation in a couple of taps.
• Explain, Simplify, Summarize and Ask the Book — spoiler-safe: the AI only sees the text up to where you are.
• Apple Intelligence runs on your device by default. Claude is optional, with your own API key.

STATS AND GOALS
• Minutes read, streaks, finished books and a daily goal.

PRIVATE
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
• Your library, highlights and progress sync through your own iCloud.
